# Planning estimates — not measured shipping data

Atitlan, Gato and Mar2 are each listed in the archived catalog as 50 × 50 cm paintings on canvas. Atitlan and Mar2 are oil; Gato is acrylic. Assume stretched canvas on a wooden stretcher, approximately 3 cm deep, without decorative frame or glass. Frame construction, depth and actual mass have not been verified.

Use the same preliminary packing model for each:

- Outer box: 62 × 62 × 14 cm (6 cm side allowance around a 50 cm canvas; space for surface clearance, corner protection, rigid panels and outer packaging).
- Artwork estimate: 1.2 kg; estimated packing: outer cardboard 1.0 kg, rigid protection 0.6 kg, cushioning/corners 0.5 kg, tape/miscellaneous 0.2 kg.
- Estimated gross: 3.5 kg. Use 4.0 kg for preliminary quote requests, with 0.5 kg contingency. These are assumptions, not material-specific measured weights.
- DHL-style dimensional-weight estimate: 62 × 62 × 14 / 5000 = 10.7632 kg. Carrier-specific rounding and tariffs determine billable weight; budget roughly an 11 kg dimensional-weight class, not a 4 kg shipment price.

No shipping budget, customs classification or verified parcel profile is inferred from these estimates. Do not book labels or pickup using these as confirmed measurements. Measure and weigh once packed; a carrier or packing service can do this. Framing/glass would require revised packing estimates.

Protect the painted face from pressure and direct contact with bubble wrap; use suitable artwork surface protection and a rigid protective system. Fully dry/cured surface and actual materials determine suitable packaging. Confirm the packing method with the carrier/packer.

- https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/how-to-ship-artwork.html
- https://www.dhlexpress.nl/en/faq/shipping/how-do-i-calculate-volumetric-weight

Owner price is USD 100 including shipping. This estimate does not establish that international delivery fits that price; eligible destination-specific quotes still determine whether an order can be accepted.
